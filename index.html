About Robo Race 2K26

Robo Race 2K26 is a robotics racing event where student teams design, build and control their own race robots. Bring your engineering skills, creativity and racing spirit.

Rules & Regulations

  • Each team can have up to 5 members.
  • Participants must provide correct registration details.
  • Robot must follow the event's technical and safety requirements.
  • Teams must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Organizers reserve the right to make final decisions during the event.
