01 · About Robo Race

Build. Think. Race.

Robo Race 2K26 is designed to challenge students to combine electronics, code, mechanics and real-time decision making into one race-ready robotic vehicle.

The Objective

Create a reliable robot, tune its control system, understand its mechanical behaviour and complete a demanding obstacle-filled track with your team.

The event encourages practical engineering, innovation and problem solving while giving participants a competitive platform to demonstrate their skills.

03 · Rules & Regulations

Know the Track Rules

Read these rules carefully before building and registering your RoboRace vehicle.

01

Team Rules

  • 4–5 members per team
  • One robot per team
  • Valid college ID mandatory
02

Robot Rules

  • Maximum size: 10 × 10 inches
  • Maximum height: 5 inches
  • Maximum voltage: 24V DC
  • Wired and wireless robots allowed
  • Readymade robots not allowed
03

Track Rules

  • Slopes
  • Sand section
  • Rollers
  • Speed breakers
  • Sharp turns
  • Slippery surfaces
04

Penalties

  • Maximum 2 hand touches
  • 5-second penalty after touch limit
  • No practice runs on final track
05

Event Rules

  • Coordinator decision is final.
